## How to read this

- Direction is worth more work only if the TimesFM IC is positive with t above ~2, beats momentum and reversal, and the top-K portfolio sits above the 95th percentile of random picks in both years, after fees.
- Volatility is worth more work only if the TimesFM range beats ATR14 and keeps a clearly positive coefficient once ATR is in the regression.
- The coins are today's survivors (picked in 2026), which flatters every long strategy here equally; compare rows, not absolute returns.
