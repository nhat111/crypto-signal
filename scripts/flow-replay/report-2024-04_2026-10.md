## How to read this

- 14 labels × 3 horizons = 42 tests: about 2 will show p < 0.05 by chance alone. A label counts only if p is small at 7 days, the sign holds at 3 and 14 days, and every year agrees.
- A spot buyer pays about 0.2% per round trip; an excess smaller than that is not a trade even if it is real.
- The 41 coins are today's survivors, chosen in 2026. Excess is measured against the same 41, so the bias cancels in that column but not in Raw.
